IAMAI

Know what a change will do before you make it.

IAMAI reads a Microsoft Entra tenant, compares it with a security baseline, and plans the rollout of that baseline while catching the pitfalls of hardening: the admin whose only method is a text message, the country rule that blocks its author, the account with no method at all. Read-only, in your browser, no account to create.

Tools

IAMAI Planner Preview

Conditional Access rollout planner

Reads the tenant's configuration and all the available sign-in evidence, compares it with a published baseline, and writes a dated plan: report-only before enforced, readiness before either, nobody locked out. Every step carries the portal clicks, the policy JSON, and the communications to send, timed to keep your people in the loop. Ships with Microsoft MVP Jon Hope's Defense in Depth baseline today; more baselines are coming, including the ability to load your own.

How these work
