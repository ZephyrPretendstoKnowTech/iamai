IAMAI

Practical tools for Microsoft Entra identity work.

Know what a change will do before you make it.

I am an identity and access engineer. These are the tools I wanted when I started: the ones that read a tenant as it actually is, say what a change would do to real people, and never touch anything.

Each one runs entirely in your browser, holds read-only permissions, and shows its working. There is no account to create and nothing to install.

Tools

How these work

  • Read-only. No tool here holds a permission that can create, change or delete anything.
  • Nothing is sent automatically. There is no server and no telemetry. What a tool reads stays on your device until you export it, and exports are redacted unless you say otherwise.
  • The source is public. Read the code, then decide whether to connect: github.com/ZephyrPretendstoKnowTech/iamai.
