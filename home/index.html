Default baseline

Defense in Depth

A baseline is the identity-security standard IAMAI measures your tenant against — here, a published set of Conditional Access policies, built by Jon Hope, a Microsoft MVP.

Its aim is layered protection for a small organisation: MFA for everyone, phishing-resistant admins, constrained sessions and devices, and emergency access that survives every policy.

IAMAI pins a reviewed version, shows you the author's changes before you take them, and can load a baseline of your own instead.

What it does with your tenant

Inspect it before you connect a tenant.

Read-only IAMAI holds no permission that can create, change or delete anything in your tenant, and lists every permission it asks for before you sign in. Nothing leaves the browser The scan reads Microsoft Graph from this browser and keeps the result here: no IAMAI server, no analytics, no upload; an export is a file you save yourself.
