IAMAI Planner Preview

Conditional Access rollout planner

  • Reads a tenant's policies, people, sign-in records and licences.
  • Compares what each baseline policy is for with what the tenant already has.
  • Writes a dated plan for the difference: report-only before enforced, who each change touches, what would break, and the emails to send.
What it catches
  • An admin whose only sign-in method is a text message, about to be required to use a passkey.
  • An emergency access account inside the policy it exists to survive.
  • A country rule that blocks the person who wrote it.
  • A service account that a legacy-authentication block stops at 2 a.m.
  • A meeting-room device that a session rule signs out every hour.
