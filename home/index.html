IAMAI

Strengthen your Microsoft identity security.

IAMAI reads your Microsoft Entra tenant, compares it with a reviewed security baseline, and writes a dated plan for closing the gaps. The plan is ordered so nobody is locked out on the way.

Read-only. The first connection needs an administrator to grant consent once; after that, Global Reader is enough.

What it does

  • Reads a tenant's policies, people, groups, devices, licences and the last 30 days of sign-in records.
  • Compares what each baseline policy is for with what the tenant already has, so a control you built yourself counts as done.
  • Plans the difference as dated steps: report-only before enforced, who each change touches, what would break, and the emails to send.

The standard it plans towards

A baseline is the identity-security standard IAMAI measures your tenant against — here, a published set of Conditional Access policies. The default is Defense in Depth, built by Jon Hope, a Microsoft MVP.

Its aim is layered protection for a small organisation: MFA for everyone, phishing-resistant admins, constrained sessions and devices, and emergency access that survives every policy.

IAMAI pins a reviewed version, shows you the author's changes before you take them, and can load a baseline of your own instead.

What it catches before a change goes live

  • An admin whose only sign-in method is a text message, about to be required to use a passkey.
  • An emergency access account inside the policy it exists to survive.
  • A country rule that blocks the person who wrote it.
  • A service account that a legacy-authentication block stops at 2 a.m.
  • A meeting-room device that a session rule signs out every hour.

What it does with your tenant

Read-only
IAMAI holds no permission that can create, change or delete anything in your tenant. It lists every permission it asks for, and what each is spent on, before you sign in.
Nothing leaves the browser
The scan reads Microsoft Graph from this browser and keeps the result here. There is no IAMAI server, no analytics and no upload; an export is a file you save yourself.
