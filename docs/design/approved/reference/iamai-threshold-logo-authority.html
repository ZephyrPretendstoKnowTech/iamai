IAMAI · final logo authority

Threshold

This is the final IAMAI mark. It is the two-panel Threshold concept selected by the owner: a shorter left panel and taller right panel form an open doorway. The two vertical columns have exactly equal horizontal widths. Their doorway-facing top edges share one height, their lower inner corners share one height, and both lower outer corners descend equally. The right-hand roof/lintel intentionally projects left of the right column; that overhang is essential to the approved doorway silhouette.

No reinterpretation. The SVG geometry below is the logo. Implementations scale this asset; they do not redraw, approximate, “clean up,” round, add a route, add a dot, add a frame, or ask an image model to reproduce it.
