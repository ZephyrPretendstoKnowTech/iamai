Precedence, highest first: production technical and content truth, then approved HTML application architecture, then this brand skin. The written contract is iamai-brand-contract.md; the machine-readable values are brand-manifest.json.

The mark

Two equal-width doorway panels, 31 units each on a 100 grid, standing either side of a 38-unit opening. The left panel slopes toward the opening; the right panel carries a leftward lintel overhang at x35,y30, a tall outer rise and a matching outward-descending lower edge. The master is src/brand/logo/iamai-threshold-master.svg, a byte-for-byte copy of the owner-approved docs/design/approved/reference/iamai-threshold-master.svg: two flat filled paths, currentColor, no stroke, no dot, no frame, no text. The right panel is five points and the lintel tip is one of them — removing it is a different mark.

Core horizontal lockup

Mark plus the word IAMAI in IBM Plex Sans 700. Gap 0.25–0.32× the mark, clear space at least 0.35× on every side, minimum standalone mark 16px, header mark 20–28px. There is no tagline, and no descriptor sits under the wordmark. The lockup is composed from the mark asset and live text, never exported as an SVG carrying <text>.

Icon-only · favicon and app icon

The favicon is the same logo on a rounded brand field, so it survives browser chrome at 16px: public/brand/favicon.svg and public/brand/favicon-32.png. A treatment, not a second logo — same geometry, scaled 0.86, #FFFDF9 on #0C6A64. No text, no tagline, no gradient.

Monochrome

Ink #1D2528 on light, light #FFFDF9 on dark, at full strength. The mark is one colour by construction, so it prints, stamps and embosses without a second file. Never screen it back: the origin node and the waypoint are the first things to go.

Typography

IBM Plex Serif for display and editorial headings, IBM Plex Sans for the interface, IBM Plex Mono for anything a person would paste into a terminal or a policy. Self-hosted Latin-1 woff2; no Google Fonts, no font CDN, no runtime third-party request (provenance and licence).

Brand colour is not status colour

This is the rule that is easiest to get wrong, and the most expensive. Teal is IAMAI: identity, navigation, selection, the interactive emphasis. Green is the tenant: proven, in place, healthy. A brand action painted in the success colour tells the reader something untrue about their own tenant.

Proven — success green

Light #0C6A64 against #2C7A5A; dark #58C8BC against #79D7A6. Two families, never one.

Icons

Restrained geometric strokes, roughly 1.75–2px at ordinary interface sizes, round caps and joins, currentColor, no fill except where a node or a state dot is the point. No emoji, no illustration, no duotone.

Control states

Focus is always visible and always the brand ring — never removed, never only a colour change. Disabled states keep AA contrast for their label.

Motion carries state and continuity, never spectacle. No looping decoration, no attention pulse, no parallax, no animated logo.
Reduced motion is not an afterthought. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every transition collapses to none, or to an opacity change of at most 60ms. Nothing that conveys state may depend on an animation having played. This page obeys its own rule.
