# Prompt 50 — Demo tenant, and the 49.1 verification findings

The last prompt of the rebuild. Part 1 closes the eight findings the 49.1 pass
turned up on GetIAMAI; Parts 2–4 replace the demo fixture with one built to show
the finished product, reached with no sign-in and with a week-two tracking story
behind `Re-scan`; Part 5 walks it and writes the docs.

Every part ran `tsc --noEmit` and the tests it touched and is its own commit; the
finishing gauntlet ran once at the end. All 922 unit tests, the smoke walk,
lint-mutations (21/21 injected violations caught), the inventory and the layout
audit are green.

## Part 1 — What the 49.1 verification found (items 1–8)

1. **The print-only header no longer renders on screen.** `.print-only` was
   `display: block`; it is now `display: none` with a `@media print` override, so
   the "IAMAI plan for … · prepared … by …" line appears only on paper.
2. **The start-date default is the next working day, computed in the tenant's
   time zone.** `nextWorkingDay(fromIso)` converts to the tenant zone, then clamps
   to a weekday; the default start and the hint now agree. Tested for a Monday, a
   Friday and a day before a freeze, in Denver and in Sydney.
3. **The passkey clause is part of the break-glass create action
   unconditionally.** The condition that dropped it on a live tenant is gone; the
   clause is now in `PREREQ.breakGlassDrill.how` for every tenant.
4. **Rows name people only when the names fit.** `whoLine` names at most two
   people and ≤28 characters, else the count (`3 people`), with the full list on
   the step; a gap suffix on a row is one shortened clause ≤40 characters
   (`shortGap`), the full sentence on the step. The step body now renders the gap
   sentence. Rows do not wrap to a third line at 760px.
5. **Blocked-by-prerequisite is a waiting state.** Its mark is `--wait`; `--stop`
   is now reserved for Skipped and for a step that would lock the operator out.
6. **Today's evidence never shows build numbers.** "Authenticator current (seen
   6.2607…)" became "Authenticator app, current"; the stale branch reads
   "Authenticator app, update available".
7. **The first click on Sign in with Microsoft starts the flow.** `signIn()`
   awaits the memoized `initAuth()` before `loginRedirect`, so a click that lands
   before MSAL's `initialize()` resolves no longer no-ops. Proven by
   `src/graph/msal.test.ts` (network-independent) and by a smoke check that the
   first click after load leaves the sign-in request in session storage.
8. **Print page 1 is the posture summary.** The cover is tenant, scan date,
   baseline, the plan's one-line header, then In place / To do / Doesn't apply by
   goal name. `Pace`, the baseline pin hash and the pace sentence are gone; pace
   presets no longer exist.

## What I could not do, and why

- **The two plan header lines read the same.** The header counts trackable and
  in-place steps; those two totals happen to match on the demo across both
  snapshots. Forcing them apart would mean changing what the header counts, which
  the surface contract fixes, so I left it: the week-two story shows in the step
  statuses and the campaign line, both asserted by tests. This is the only stated
  deliverable that did not come out visibly distinct, and it is called out here
  rather than papered over.
- **The item-7 smoke check needs network reach to the sign-in authority.**
  MSAL builds the authorize URL from `login.microsoftonline.com` metadata before
  it navigates, so the smoke's "first click starts the flow" check assumes CI can
  reach it (it can). The network-independent proof of the fix is the unit test.
