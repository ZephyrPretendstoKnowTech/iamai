# Pass 2B — Implementation Tasks and their channels: the words

## What was read

45 steps appear in `docs/qa/tile-dump.txt`; 42 of them have a content package under
`docs/implementation-content/`, and 5 (`s-goal-all-users-no-persistence`, the four
`s-review-baseline-*`) draw their procedure from `src/roadmap/portalLines.ts`. The compiled
registry `src/content/implementation/registry.generated.json` holds **939 blocks / 644,182
characters**: 346 Entra, 218 AI Info, 190 JSON, 58 Email, 39 PowerShell, 46 readiness, 42
troubleshooting. Every Email block and every AI Info block was read whole; every Entra block was
read whole; JSON and PowerShell were read for their prose (bodies, header comments, `#` lines)
rather than line-by-line code, which is pass 2A's. 41 distinct Implementation Task names were
enumerated from the `tasks:` lines. All quotes below were re-read from the current tree.

## 2. Channels or tasks that should not exist

1. **`ai.blocked` on ~20 policy packages** — the job it fails: an AI Info block exists to give an
   assistant the facts the page cannot fit. These carry only `{{dependencies.blockers}}` and
   "resolve these before creating or changing the policy", which is the step card verbatim. No
   artifact signal can show it did a job the card already did. Delete, or replace with the one
   thing the card cannot hold — the intended target and why the blocker gates it.
2. **`email.enforce` on the thirteen packages where it is byte-identical to `email.rollout`** —
   two artifacts, one text, at two different moments. Delete the duplicate and project the one
   email at both states, or write the enforcement announcement the state deserves.
3. **`ai.not-licensed` as nine separate blocks** — the text differs only in the policy name, and
   six of the nine make a claim the scan cannot support. One shared block with a bound name.
4. **The duplicated `whyIamAISaysThis.sections.whyItMatters` key in 24 readiness blocks** — a
   second copy of a sentence that is already in the block. Nothing reads both.
5. **`s-goal-session-lifetime`'s five `entra.correct.unmanaged.*` blocks and its two
   `policies.session.unmanaged.*` removed-exclusion lines** — the package states twice that no
   second policy exists. Either they are dead, or the package's own claim is false; both readings
   say these blocks should not be in their current form.

## 4. Patterns — one commit per class

1. **Numbers hardcoded into fragments that are conditionally assembled.** `composeByMismatch`
   (and the passkey package's block sequence) picks a subset and renders it; the numerals were
   written assuming everything renders. Six policy packages plus `s-prereq-passkey-settings`.
   *One fix:* strip the numerals from block text and number at projection time. This is the
   highest-yield single change in the corpus.
2. **A warning repeated once per fragment instead of once per procedure.** "Keep the policy's
   current state. If it is On, the changed rule can affect access after you save." is in 48
   Entra blocks, up to four in a single assembled task, plus the AI Info and the script comment.
   "This change removes … from the policy's exclusions" is in 78 blocks. *One fix:* both belong
   to the assembled region, not to each fragment.
3. **The step's title reused as the task name, the email subject and the script title.** Seventeen
   tasks, fifty-eight email subjects, nine script headers. Where the title is also *wrong* for the
   channel it becomes severity 4 (`workload-identity-block`). *One fix:* one authored
   `task.title` verb phrase per step; emails take an audience-shaped subject; scripts take the
   package title from `META.json`.
4. **Two spellings of the same state.** `notLicensed` is written six times as a fact about the
   tenant and three times as a limit of the scan; `s-verify-mfa` says IAMAI both holds and does
   not hold the snooze value; `session-lifetime` says both that there is one policy and that there
   are two. Every one of these is a place where two sources exist for one fact. *One fix:* a
   single authored sentence per state, bound, reused.
5. **Three AI Info dialects.** Prose, ALL-CAPS sections, and `ROLE`/`GOAL`. Two packages switch
   dialect between states of one task. *One fix:* pick the prose form and convert the six caps
   packages; the caps content survives as paragraphs.
6. **Directive-only blocks.** "Explain …", "Do not …", "say that it is unknown rather than
   treating the prerequisite as met" — instructions to an assistant sitting in text an admin
   reads, and in four `s-prereq-passkey-settings` blocks and one `s-goal-inforcer-mfa` block they
   are the *entire* content. *One fix:* separate the prompt's instructions from the tenant facts;
   a channel whose whole body is orders has no job.
7. **Invariant paragraphs duplicated across every state of a package.** PIM ×7, session-lifetime
   ×4, intune-enrollment ×4, device-registration ×4. *One fix:* a package preamble the projection
   emits once.
8. **The honest-scan caution, reworded fifteen times.** The best content in the product, stated as
   fifteen near-synonyms, four of them ungrammatical. *One fix:* one sentence with a bound
   subject.
9. **PowerShell in two registers.** Nine scripts documented and readable; thirty undocumented and
   minified. *One fix:* generate the four header lines from `META.json` and format uniformly.
10. **Email written from the admin's seat, not the recipient's.** Step-title subjects, internal
    object names, unexplained jargon (GDAP, B2B, "sign-in frequency", "the approved set"),
    unstated numbers ("the new frequency"), hedged disruption ("may be unavailable"), and
    inconsistent salutation/signature (`{{signature}}` / hardcoded "Thanks, IT" / nothing).
    *One fix:* one email template — audience, what changes, what you will notice, what to do —
    and rewrite the fifty-eight bodies into it.

## 5. Not checked, and why

- **PowerShell and JSON bodies as code.** Read for prose only; whether a `param` block or a Graph
  body is correct is pass 2A's.
- **Rendered composition.** Findings about assembled order (numbering, repeated warnings) were
  derived from `projection.mode: "composeByMismatch"` in the registry, not from a running page;
  the audit brief forbids the walk and smoke, and `tile-dump.txt` records the `tasks:` line but
  not the assembled procedure body.
- **`troubleshooting` (42 blocks) and `manual` (2)** were sampled, not read whole — they are not
  among the five channels this pass owns.
- **Steps only the `mid` fixture builds** were read from the registry, not from a rendered page.
