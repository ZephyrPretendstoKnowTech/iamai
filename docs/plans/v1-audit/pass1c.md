# Pass 1C — every Tasks Remaining card, read as the person who inherits the plan

Read `docs/qa/tile-dump.json` (the twin of `docs/qa/tile-dump.txt`, generated by
`scripts/tile-dump.mjs` from `stepBody.ts` / `policyTasks.ts` / `emergencyReadiness.ts`):
**274 step renderings, 45 distinct steps, 815 cards (221 distinct by content) across 8 fixtures**
— `demo`, `demo-week2`, `small`, `mid`, `large`, `messy`, `midflight`, `hostile`. 243 cards are
satisfied, 572 open. Every quote below is from that dump; causes named in the "proposed fix"
column were read in the current tree. Nothing was edited.

Two facts frame everything else, both measured over all 815 cards:

- **9 cards of 815 mention a date, an age or a scan time** — and all nine are the same sentence
  on `s-prereq-per-user-mfa` ("On the day Require MFA for Everyone enforces…"), which is a future
  instruction, not a record. **No card anywhere says when a check passed.**
- **0 cards of 815 distinguish a person's saved answer from a scan reading.** No card contains
  "you chose", "your decision", "saved answer", "recorded" or any equivalent. The one exception is
  a completed-check string, `Core - Exclusions · Selection: Saved`.

## 4. Patterns — fix these once, everywhere

1. **A passed check deletes its own evidence.** `emergencyReadiness.ts:~76`
   (`instruction: satisfied ? '' : direction ?? tile.note ?? ''`) blanks `tile.note` the moment a
   tile turns `good`. Every producer that computes a careful caveat — "clues, not proof of
   dedicated use", "location-based exceptions are not applied", "IAMAI uses that existing object
   automatically" — has it thrown away at the point it becomes the permanent record. **Thirteen
   distinct satisfied cards are completely empty because of this one line.** Fixing it alone turns
   most of §2 from "delete the card" into "the card is now useful".

2. **One vocabulary, two meanings.** `policyTasks.ts:308` puts the *next* rung in the heading of
   an open card and the *current* state in the heading of a satisfied one. "Report-only",
   "Ready to enforce" and "Enforced" therefore each mean either "this is where it is" or "this is
   where it goes next", decided by a `satisfied` flag the card never shows. Nothing else in the
   product causes as many misreadings.

3. **Nothing is dated.** 815 cards, zero timestamps. Every "In place", "Verified", "Configured",
   "Membership verified", "Exact match found" is an undated assertion. The audit's own bar demands
   a dated source line on procedures; the cards never got one.

4. **A person's choice is drawn as a scan finding.** Zero cards mark provenance. "Everyone is
   remote", "Put this step back if you want it on the plan again", the whole Direction set and the
   trusted-location mapping are all operator answers rendered in the same grey as a Graph read.

5. **Completed checks are state redrawn, not history.** On policy cards they are the ladder up to
   the current rung; on `s-prereq-exclusion-group` they are mode readings of *other* policies; on
   `s-prereq-break-glass` they are standing properties of the account. None of them record an
   event, so none of them answer "what did my predecessor actually do?".

6. **A claim and its own contradiction sit on one step.** `Enforced` + "no policy for IAMAI to
   write"; `Enforced` + "Application not established"; "In place" + "when 1 trusted location
   exists (now 0)"; "Needs Correction: NZ is left out" + "No named location matches Work
   Countries". In each case two producers write to one step and neither reads the other.

7. **Unread evidence is drawn as a pass.** `hostile`'s exact reach numbers, `s-check-dormant-accounts`'s
   "No user impact", `s-prereq-passkey-settings`'s In-place step with an unreadable-methods card.
   The conservative branches exist in the code (`peopleUnknown`, `outcome: 'unknown'`); the card
   layer does not always reach them. Silence reads as fine.

8. **State changes between snapshots are invisible.** Every demo → demo-week2 flip (7 steps
   changed state, 20 cards changed or vanished) is silent: no "changed since the last scan", no
   before/after, no date. The two-snapshot demo is the product's own demonstration of the
   inheritor's problem, and it demonstrates that the cards cannot answer it.

9. **Four words for "this passed"** — `In place`, `Verified`, `Configured`, `Ready` (plus
   `Enforced`, `Exact match found`, `Required references present`, `Membership verified`) — and two
   casings for the same subject on the same step. The Emergency Access bar is one vocabulary; the
   rest of the product has eight.

10. **The card's one sentence is the step's sentence, not the subject's.** `policyTasks.ts:315`
    sets `detail: contract.whatToDo.text` for every member card, which is why "Finish the steps
    this one waits on first." and "Confirm and save the required decision." appear under headings
    that contradict them.
