## Decided today (owner, 2026-09-25)

### Controls are exact

- A step completes only when every condition, grant and session setting equals the policy the plan writes. Only the name may differ, and a Policy Name card says so.
- Code: `tracking.ts` compares with nothing judged; creates are compared against their whole body.
- The tenant's exclusions group and its own authentication context stand in for the baseline author's (`observation.ts withTenantContext`).
- Graph metadata is never a difference: `@odata.*` keys, and `secureAppSessionMode: 'notEnforced'` (`observation.ts material`, `UNSET_DEFAULTS`).
- A tenant's own single delivering policy is compared for its own step (`generate.ts ownStep`).
- Create the Policies in Report-only lists a policy with a differing setting as "Correct {fields}".
- When a delivered step is reopened for a correction:
  - it leaves its wave (`forecast.ts settleForecast`);
  - it drops its finished reading;
  - if it waits on a foundation it reads Blocked (`foundations.ts`), and its milestone names the row's reason (`lifecycle.ts correctionGate`).

### Policies doing another step's job

- The owner chose this narrow scope over "never edit any tenant policy". The broad version is abandoned; don't rebuild it.
- A policy with a grant is never tied to, or corrected for, a step whose plan policy has no grant (a session-only step). That step creates the baseline's policy.
- Code: `generate.ts` and `tracking.ts`, `anotherStepsJob`, `anotherJobOnly`.

### Never weaken

These count as "stricter", are accepted, and are noted on a Stricter Than the Baseline card (`MemberTracking.stricter`):
- a grant or session at least as strict as the floor;
- wider users with no extra exclusions (`tracking.ts usersWider`).

### Accept this difference (deviations, option B)

- On a step with a difference, the person gives a reason. It saves as a step decision under `deviation:<stepId>` (`decisions.ts deviationDecision`, `MappingState.acceptedDeviations`), with each setting's fingerprint.
- The step completes with an Accepted Difference card and reopens if an accepted setting changes.
- The control is `ContentStep.tsx AcceptDeviation`.

### Other rules from today

- Intune Enrollment is never credited to a risk policy: the goal signature has `noRisk`, and `tracking.ts riskOnlyFor`.
- "Nothing left to do" is never a line (`stepContract.ts isAllClear`).
- Create the Policies in Report-only lists every policy's create procedure, whether created or not.
- Section 6 finishes on the section completion form ("IAMAI sees {policy} On…" plus its Report-only period), as sections 4 and 5 do (`isSectionPolicy`).

### Test fixture

- `src/roadmap/fixtures/asPlanned.ts` builds one step's policy exactly as the plan asks. Use it in tests whose premise is "the policy is as planned".
- Never build fixture data through the engine: the fixtures module would pull the Graph client into the demo chunk (`demo.test.ts` A).
