- **Block Risky Users From Registering Sign-in Methods.** Jon's RiskyUsers-RegisterSecurityInfo, a standing P2 control with nothing like it in the plan. A new step after 5.7: a new goal and a goal-map re-pin. About 2–3 hours.
- **EAM High-Risk Users.** Jon's companion to Remediate Risky Users for tenants using an external MFA provider. It becomes a second policy in 5.7 when external methods are detected. Keep the scan's external-methods detection when the Direction question retires. About 2 hours.
- **The lockdown kit.** Jon's three ZTCA incident-response switches (All Apps × 2, Admin Portal), created ahead and never switched on, with a runbook. The owner decided this on 19 Sep. Open question: create them in Report-only or Off. About 3–4 hours.
- **The "In the baseline, not in this plan" group on the printed plan.** The screen has it in v1.0 (753885ad).
- **Specific reasons for the footer rows that fall back to "No step in this plan covers it for this tenant."** These are:
  - the two AGENT policies;
  - the withheld ZTCA Admin Portal block (this reason is misleading for it, because it is withheld on every tenant);
  - the Service Accounts and EntraConnectIDSync workload blocks, where they don't apply.
  Each needs owner-approved wording.
- **The footer's prose budget in docs/qa/page-contracts.json.** It allows 16 sentences / 260 words, and the demo footer with this group is now about 26 / 419. Two rows run past 25 words. The owner decides: raise the budget or shorten the reasons. The walk reads it; CI doesn't.
- **Uploaded baselines.** Footer reasons are matched by policy name, so a similarly named policy in an uploaded baseline would pick up a pinned policy's reason.
- **Gate the countries policy's turn-on on its lockout warnings.** In v1.0 only the empty-list check (cty.atLeastOne) holds 6.3's turn-on; the other three (includesOperator, seenCountriesIncluded, unknownCountries) draw as Needs Correction and hold nothing (owner, 2026-09-23). The probe case: allowedCountries ['NZ'] while an admin signs in from AU.
