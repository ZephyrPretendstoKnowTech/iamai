The view

What changes on the Plan page before any step moves.

  • All work opens first.You said people are less jarred when a shrunken view is something they choose. Jordan sees at a glance that Emergency Access is unfinished; Marcus reads one list top to bottom; Nadia sees the whole journey from the start.
  • Sections never move. Numbered 1–8, in place from the first scan to the last. Nothing is lifted above the tabs, sunk to the bottom, or hidden. The countries jump you hit happened because a finished Direction had sunk to the bottom. With Direction fixed at section 2, every “Answered in” link points up, to something already answered.
  • Finished work shrinks in place. A Completed step becomes one line (number, title, Completed, date); click to open. A fully finished section becomes its heading, e.g. “1. Establish Emergency Access · 4 of 4 completed”. Deferred rows shrink the same way. You see what's done and what's left in one picture, and done work doesn't fill the page. Sam keeps finished work at hand to re-check; Priya scrolls less.
  • Show completed and Show deferred go. All work already shows everything, so the two toggles have nothing left to do.
  • Tabs stay as filters you choose: All work (default), Ready, Up Next, On Hold. Each keeps the same headings, order and numbers; a section with nothing in that tab hides; numbers never shift, so a gap means the row is in another tab. Up Next versus On Hold is a distinction you decided on (waiting for a step above, versus held on a person or on evidence). With the new order, an Up Next row's reason always points up the page.
  • Opening a step from a link or tile keeps you on your tab if the step is in it; otherwise it switches to All work and scrolls to the step. Today it drops you into the step's lane tab, which is the disjointed feeling you described.
  • Header tiles filter the one list, so each section heading appears once. Today a tile can draw the same heading three times, once per lane.

I read “minimized tabs for items already completed” as completed items shown minimized inside All work, not as a separate Completed tab.

  11. Follow Jon's layers exactly.Rejected He publishes four layers and one rule, not a step order (his wiki is empty). Strict layers would put the countries block in the core, where it can't turn on before defaults go off, and risk after devices. His one rule is kept.
