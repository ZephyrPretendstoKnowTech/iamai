# 50.1 — The plan is derived, never stored

Precondition: 50 committed and green (9720153). Read `docs/design/target-state.md` and
`docs/qa/page-contracts.json` in full. Neither is yours to edit.

Found on the 50 verification, on GetIAMAI and on the demo. Six items; the first is the
root cause of two of the others and of three "verified on the fixture, absent on the
live tenant" reports in a row.

## Part 1 — The plan record stores decisions only

1. Today the Plan renders a persisted plan record: step text, populations, evidence
   lines and schedule are read from IndexedDB as generated by whichever build first
   made them. On GetIAMAI the emergency-access create action still lacks the passkey
   clause three prompts after it was added; on the demo, Re-scan swaps the snapshot —
   Today moves from 12 to 15 proven — and the Plan does not change a row.
   The record now holds decisions only: skipped step ids, ticked Done-when facts, the
   start date and freeze, the assumption answers, checkpoints. Everything else — steps,
   statuses, populations, evidence lines, dates, the header line — is regenerated from
   the current snapshot, the decisions and the current code on every load, re-scan and
   edit. The plan file (Export) keeps its v2 shape by writing the regenerated plan
   plus the decisions; on load it takes the decisions and regenerates.
2. Migration: an existing record is read once for its decisions and rewritten in the
   new shape; the old fields are dropped. A test loads a pre-50.1 record and asserts
   the plan renders from the snapshot, not from the record's cached text.
3. Tests that exercise a UI path ("verified: the clause is present") run against a
   rendered surface on the mock, never against a fixture-only function, so a cached
   record can't make a green test lie again. The smoke asserts the passkey clause on
   the rendered emergency-access step.

## Part 2 — Readiness-held steps render

4. On the demo, five applicable goals — legacy auth, device code, MFA for everyone,
   admins phishing-resistant, guests MFA — appear nowhere: no row, not in place, not
   doesn't-apply. The header's "3 MFA steps wait for MFA readiness" refers to them. A
   step held by a readiness threshold renders as a Blocked row in the wave it would
   join, with its date column reading the reason in the 46 shape: `when MFA readiness
   reaches 90% (now 40%)`. The header's step count includes them; the property test
   "every step renders exactly one row or one footer line" is added to every fixture.
5. On the demo's week-two snapshot, the exclusions-group step is done and two Wave 1
   policies are in report-only; after Part 1 the rows and the header must show it. The
   smoke asserts the day-one and week-two Plans differ in the expected rows and that
   the header's "in place" count rises.

## Part 3 — The dependency rule

6. Housekeeping on the demo flags `Core - Block - Legacy authentication` and `Core -
   Block - Device code flow` for excluding none of the sign-in dependencies. Both are
   correctly narrowed by a client-app or authentication-flow condition and are the two
   most standard policies in existence; flagging them costs trust. The rule fires only
   on a block policy whose scope is not already narrowed by a client-app, auth-flow,
   platform, device-filter or location condition. Tests for each condition.

## Part 4 — Sign-in, entry, and a clause

7. The first click on `Sign in with Microsoft` after a page load still does nothing on
   the live site; the second works. The mock test passed because the mock authority
   answers instantly. Make the button disabled-with-spinner until MSAL's
   `initialize()` has resolved and the authority metadata is loaded, then enabled;
   a click before that is queued and fires when ready. Test against the real
   authority metadata fetch in the smoke's item-7 check, not the mock.
8. `See it with sample data →` on Connect: an extension click on the anchor did not
   navigate; direct navigation to its href works. Make it an ordinary anchor with a
   full page load — no router interception — and assert in the smoke that a click on
   it lands on the demo Plan with the banner.
9. Gap clauses on rows are shortened, not truncated: `sessions expire every 168h,
   baseline wants 4h`, never `…persist in the…`. A per-goal short clause, ≤40
   characters, beside the full sentence in the coverage output.
