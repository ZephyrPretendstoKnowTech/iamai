# Pre-share blockers

Larger than the audit prompt allowed, or impossible without a live tenant or a live
deploy. One line of impact each; every line needs a decision before the tool is shared.

## Closed

| Item | What was built | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The free-tier ladder was not part of the plan for an unlicensed tenant: a tenant with no Entra ID P1 got a two-step plan. | Without `entraP1` the ladder **is** the plan. `src/roadmap/ladder.ts` turns each item in `data/free-tier-ladder.json` into a phase 0 step in ladder order, with copy in `src/copy/ladder.ts`: exact portal instructions, an exit criterion, its own verification and rollback, a plain title, a manager sentence, and a per-tenant impact from what a free licence can read (security defaults, the authentication methods policy, role assignments and their holders by name, guests and unaccepted invitations, licence assignment). A rung reads Done only where the tenant proves it and names that evidence; where Graph does not expose the setting at all (app passwords, per-user MFA state) the step says so rather than guessing. The same gate removes what only a policy would use: no exclusion group, no trusted location, no allowed-countries location, and never "turn security defaults off" when nothing could take their place. Rungs carry `ladder: true`, so they read "Hardening step", not "Prerequisite", and are never called groundwork for a later step. | `src/roadmap/ladder.test.ts` (12 tests: data and copy agree, every rung has instructions and an exit criterion, Done always names evidence, the verdicts per readable setting, what Graph does not expose is said plainly, a covering phase 0 step takes the rung's place and position, ladder order is the plan order, a licensed tenant gets none). Smoke walks the unlicensed plan and opens a rung. `docs/screens/blockers/free-tier-plan.png`, `free-tier-step.png`. The mock plan goes from 2 steps to 12. |
| A non-admin or Global Reader sign-in degraded sections with Graph's own 403 text and never named the missing role. | `src/graph/collect/roles.ts` maps every scope in the registry to the roles that grant it for a delegated read, least-privilege first. The Scan page names the role per refused section, says Global Reader grants everything IAMAI reads and writes nothing, and links Microsoft's role reference. Denials are read from the live scan and from the saved scan, so the advice survives a reload. A refused section is no longer labelled "not available on this licence": that wording is a licence gate only. The "What IAMAI reads" page and `SPEC.md` §4 gained a **Least role** column, generated from the same map. | `src/graph/collect/roles.test.ts` (7 tests: every registry scope maps to a role, Global Reader covers all of them, the least-privilege answer is always a reader role, a licence reason is never read as a missing role). Smoke walks `?dev=1&mock=1&denied=1`. `docs/screens/blockers/scan-role-refused.png`, `reads-least-role.png`. |
