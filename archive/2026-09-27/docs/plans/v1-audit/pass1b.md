## 5. Patterns — fix the class, not the card

1. **The stage track outranks the hold.** A step's milestone stage (`Report-only → Ready to enforce → Enforced`) is promoted to the card's bold check line regardless of whether the step is blocked, needs correction, or has no policy at all. This produces all three severity-4 contradictions above (18 "Enforced with no policy", 3 "Enforced / Needs correction", 1 "Ready to enforce / keep it in Report-only") and 24 open cards whose check word is `Enforced`. **One fix**: the check line is the next *unmet* thing; the stage may only be the check line when nothing is open.
2. **The pass word describes the step's goal, not the card's subject.** `Security defaults · In place` (= off), `Trusted network · In place` (= exists), `Per-user MFA states · Not in place` (= still enforced). Three cards, two words, three different relations to the named subject. Every "In place / Not in place" should be read against its own heading and replaced with a word true of that subject.
3. **`detail` is dropped on passing configuration-finding cards.** `generate.ts` writes a detail for every finding; `policySubjectsOf` renders it only when the card is open or its sentence is not already said. Result: 26 `Existing coverage`, 7 `Authentication strength · Ready`, 7 `Trusted Network · Everyone is remote`, 5 `Exclusions · Ready` — subject and check only, no information. Either render `detail` or do not draw the card.
4. **The card kind is the heading; the subject is buried.** `Prerequisites`, `Decision`, `Conditional Access policy`, `Preparation step` head cards whose actual subject (which prerequisite, which question, which policy) is in the body — the inverse of the Emergency Access anatomy. Directly causes the indistinguishable-pairs faults: two `Prerequisites` on one step, two `Waiting on your direction`, two `Allowed countries`, two `Trusted Network`. `content.json` already holds `pages.app.plan.blockedSubject.*` for exactly this; the cards do not use it as the heading.
5. **Blocker bindings written as badge suffixes are used as card sentences.** `when 1 Temporary Access Pass policy exists (now 0)`, `when people without a method reaches 0 (now 7)`, `after: Identify the Inforcer application` — lowercase, verbless, sometimes with a dangling colon. They were written to follow "Blocked · ". Every `blocked.*` binding needs a sentence form.
6. **The step bar does not read the lane.** `Complete the next task shown for each item.` sits above 120 held renderings, above a step with `tasks: (none)` and above five deferred steps. One producer, one rule: the bar states what the lane permits.
7. **Two counters, one word.** "N checks remaining" counts real checks on the Emergency Access cards and milestones on the policy cards. Same for guest counts (directory vs affected), `active people` vs `covers N enabled`, and readiness (`68%` on one step, `not measured` on the next). Wherever the product shows a number, it must name the denominator.
8. **Unknowns are stated honestly and then abandoned.** `Not confirmed`, `Confirm no legitimate use`, `Unknown`, `Application not established`, `Not established`, `Not fully read` — 28 `Affected people · Not established` alone. The conservatism is right and is the product's best quality, but a check that names no way to close it reads as a defect rather than as care. Every unknown card needs either an action, a way to record a human confirmation, or a sentence saying it will stay open by design.
9. **Repetition between card and card.** `s-goal-intune-enrollment-reauth` says "Review MFA before enabling Every time" three times on one screen; `s-goal-guests-mfa` states the same external-user/home-organization/exclusions caveat on two adjacent cards in different words; `s-goal-service-accounts-trusted-network` names its two missing objects on three cards. `policySubjectsOf` already de-duplicates the *policy* card's sentence against the others (line 349-350); the rule needs to apply between every pair of cards.
10. **Portal vocabulary drifts.** `Temporary Access Pass policy` (TAP is a method in the Authentication methods policy), `Blocked` on a Conditional Access policy card (collides with the Block grant control), `Enforced` as a milestone beside `Report-only` and `Mode: On` as a completed check (three vocabularies for policy state, only two of which exist in the portal), `Trusted network` as a subject (the portal has named locations marked as trusted). An experienced admin reads vocabulary drift as inexperience.
11. **Mixed capitalisation, two conventions per column.** Title Case subjects (`Administrator Account Evidence`, `Guest Directory`) beside sentence case (`Policy exclusions`); Title Case checks (`Needs Correction`, `Not Fully Read`) beside lowercase (`not measured`).
